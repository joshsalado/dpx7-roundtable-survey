How to Use the Roundtable Dashboard

1️⃣
Run the Roundtable
Use the 20-min facilitation guide with 5-8 AAs. Focus on low-scoring Connections areas.
2️⃣
Show the QR Code
At the end, have AAs scan the QR code. It takes 30 seconds and is 100% anonymous.
3️⃣
Track Results Here
Come back to this dashboard to see scores, trends, and written feedback over time.

Tips for Leaders

  • Keep it casual — this is a conversation, not a lecture
  • Don't defend or explain decisions — just listen and take notes
  • Follow up on action items before starting new topics
  • Target 6-12 month tenure group first (biggest gap: 15% below benchmark)
  • Aim for groups of 6-10 AAs for best participation

20-Minute Roundtable Agenda

0-3 min — Welcome
Set the Tone
"Thanks for being here. This is your time — everything said here stays here. No wrong answers. We want to make DPX7 a better place to work."
3-8 min — Open Check-In
How Are Things Going?
"On a scale of 1-4, how would you rate your experience at DPX7 this past week? What went well? What could be better?"
8-15 min — Deep Dive
Focus on Key Themes
"What's one new skill you'd like to learn here?" / "How's your schedule flexibility?" / "Do you feel leaders are approachable?"
15-18 min — Action Commitment
What Can We Do?
"Based on what we discussed, here's what I'm going to follow up on: [list items]. Anything else?"
18-20 min — Close & Survey
QR Code Feedback
"Please scan this QR code to give us quick anonymous feedback. It takes 30 seconds. Thank you — your voice matters."
