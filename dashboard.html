Tips for Leaders
  • Rotate AAs each week so everyone gets a turn

20-Minute Roundtable Agenda

Follow this script to facilitate each weekly roundtable. Click each section to expand.

0-3 min
Welcome & Set the Tone
SAY SOMETHING LIKE:
"Hey everyone, thanks for being here. This is a small-group conversation. I want to hear what's really on your mind about working at DPX7. There are no wrong answers. Your Connections scores told us there are areas we need to improve, and I need your help figuring out how."
Key points:
• This is anonymous/safe - no retaliation
• You genuinely want feedback, not just checking a box
• Their input directly shapes leadership actions
3-8 min
Open-Ended Check-In
ASK:
"On a scale of 1-4, how are you feeling about work right now - and what's the #1 reason behind that number?"
• Go around the table, everyone gets ~30-40 seconds
• Listen actively, don't defend or explain - just acknowledge
• Take notes on themes

Follow-up probes:
• "What's one thing that would move that number up by one point?"
• "Is that something you've felt for a while, or did something change recently?"
8-15 min
Deep Dive on Key Themes
Pick 1-2 themes based on what surfaced in the check-in:
SKILL DEVELOPMENT (25% want more)
"What skills would you most want to learn? Are there roles or tasks you'd like to try?"
SCHEDULING (19% want better)
"How does your current schedule affect your life outside work? If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
LEADER SUPPORT (14% want more)
"What does 'support from leaders' mean to you? What would you need more of day-to-day?"
THE "SOMETHING ELSE" (35%)
"A lot of teammates said the biggest improvement isn't on our standard list. What's the thing we're not asking about?"
Facilitation tips:
• Let silence sit - don't rush to fill it
• If one person dominates: "That's great - [Name], what's your take?"
• Validate: "I hear you on that" / "That makes sense"
15-18 min
Action Commitment
SAY:
"Based on what I've heard today, here's what I'm taking away. I want to commit to [specific action] and I'll update you on progress by [date]."
• Commit to ONE concrete action you can deliver within 2 weeks
• Be specific (not "we'll look into it" - instead "I'll talk to ops about [X] by Friday")
• Set a visible follow-up date
18-20 min
Close & QR Code Survey
SAY:
"Thank you for being open with me today. Before you go, scan this QR code for a 30-second anonymous feedback survey on whether this session was helpful."
• Click the QR Code tab on this dashboard
• Let AAs scan with their phone cameras
• Close with: "I'll be doing these every week with different groups. My door is always open."
Do
• Listen more than you talk (80/20)
• Take written notes on themes
• Follow up on commitments publicly
• Rotate participants weekly
Don't
• Get defensive or make excuses
• Promise things you can't deliver
• Single anyone out after
• Let it run over 20 minutes
